Keep the AI chat session model for decomposition. It's easier to communicate and make adjustments with an AI conversationally than through a structured-only UI.

**Why:** The back-and-forth nature of decomposition refinement maps well to conversation. Structured controls (DAG, split/merge) should augment the chat, not replace it.

**How to apply:** The decomposition review UI should have the chat rail as a primary interaction method, with structured visualization alongside it. Don't design decomposition as a form-filling exercise.
