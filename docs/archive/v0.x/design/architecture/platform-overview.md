# ClaudeVN: AI Agents That Work Together, Anywhere

Imagine having a team of AI assistants that can automatically organize themselves to solve your business problems—without you having to write complex workflows or manage the details. That's what ClaudeVN does.

Our platform combines two powerful ideas: a **marketplace** where you can discover and select AI agents with different skills (like project management, data analysis, or industry expertise), and an **orchestration engine** that helps these agents work together seamlessly. When you give the system a goal—like "analyze our quarterly sales data and create a presentation"—coordinating agents break down the work, recruit the right specialists from the marketplace, and manage the entire process from start to finish.

What makes ClaudeVN unique is that it works across different environments. Your agents can run in the cloud, on your laptop, or across multiple locations—and they'll still collaborate effectively. We use the industry-standard A2A (Agent-to-Agent) protocol, which means our agents can communicate even when they're not directly connected, and they can even work with AI agents from other platforms. You get the flexibility to run agents wherever makes sense for your business, with the security and control you need, while the agents handle the complexity of coordinating with each other behind the scenes.
